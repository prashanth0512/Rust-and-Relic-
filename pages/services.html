The Sourcing Network

How we find the world's most elusive fashion treasures.

01

Handpicked Global Sourcing

Our network spans across European flea markets, private estate sales, and exclusive archive collectors. We don't just buy; we hunt for pieces with soul and history.

02

Provenance & Authenticity Checks

Every item undergoes a rigorous 12-point inspection. We verify labels, stitching techniques, and material composition to ensure 100% authenticity and documented history.

03

Condition Grading & Restoration

Items are graded from 'Museum Quality' to 'Excellent Wearable'. Our in-house artisans perform museum-grade restoration on delicate textiles when necessary.
