Preserving Fashion History

Rust & Relic is more than a vintage boutique—it is a living archive of style, craftsmanship, and soul.

Every thread we rescue tells a story. We scour European flea markets, private estate sales, and collector archives to curate rare, high-quality vintage garments that stand the test of time. By breathing new life into these historic pieces, we advocate for a more sustainable, circular fashion future.

From 1960s silk gowns to distressed selvedge denim and varsity jackets, each piece is meticulously verified, graded, and restored by our in-house curators. Welcome to the archive—where the beauty of the past meets the wardrobe of your future
